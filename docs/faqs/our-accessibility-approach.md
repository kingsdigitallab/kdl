Accessibility is integrated into our Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) during each site's design and development. We identify requirements early, distribute responsibilities across roles, and use refined documentation shaped by project trials to ensure accessibility is embedded rather than added late. This approach balances inclusive design, practical sustainability, and ongoing team feedback, enabling us to deliver accessible, maintainable digital outputs as standard practice.

During the development process, and particularly towards the end of the (SDLC), each website will be assessed for digital accessibility using the KCL instance of SiteImprove, and other testing tools, to detect WCAG issues and produce an Accessibility Statement compliant with the College standard for Disability Support and Inclusion.
